Outline: righteous Asura king, grandson of Prahlada, prosperous no crime/poverty; Devas threatened, appeal to Vishnu; Vishnu as Vamana dwarf Brahmin boy; asks 3 paces; grows cosmic, measures worlds; Mahabali offers his head for third step; (Bhagavata: Shukra warns; Garuda binds with Varuna's noose; Bali honoured, rules Sutala); Kerala: allowed yearly visit on Thiruvonam; customs pookkalam, Onasadya, Thrikkakara link. Historicity debated (Maduraikkanji, Thiruvalla plate 861 AD).
Sensitivities: Vamana is Vishnu (religious framing); Mahabali beloved king in Kerala vs Asura lineage; do not call him a villain; don't assert historicity (2022 and 2016 political controversies); colonial-era sources carry bias; trick-reading of Vamana.
