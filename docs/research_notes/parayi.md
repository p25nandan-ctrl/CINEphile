Match: standard title; Aithihyamala Part 1 ch.6 (1909). Summary flag: "each growing into a different craft" only partly accurate (roles include ritual, soldier, bard, temple servant, one daughter, and a deity; the twelfth, Vayillakkunnilappan, is not adopted). Suggest "different calling"; summary NOT changed. Vararuchi identity/dating disputed.

Outline: Vararuchi, Brahmin scholar at Vikramaditya's court; answers king's question after overhearing divine beings under a tree; Parayi-born girl set adrift (raft+lamp vs box+nail); raised by Brahmin household/found; Vararuchi marries her; on travels she bears twelve children, each left; eleven raised by families of different castes/occupations; twelfth "mouthless" child (mother's claim) set on hill as deity Vayillakkunnilappan. Twelve (varies by source): Mezhathol Agnihotri, Rajakan, Perumthachan, Vallon, Panan, Narayana Bhranthan, Akavoor Chathan, Karakkal Amma, Uppukkottan, Vaduthala Nair, Pakkanar, Vayillakkunnilappan. Sources disagree on details.
Sensitivities: caste premise; legend not history; don't blame the mother; careful with Parayan/Parayi terms; infant abandonment, mouthless child (grades 8-10, gentle); Uppukkottan Muslim trader; avoid dating; teacher note.
